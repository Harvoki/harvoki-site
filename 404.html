How to play

A cozy life on the island, with real rewards.

Farm

Till, plant and water. Crops grow by season and wet soil grows faster.

Raise animals

Build coops and barns, feed your animals, collect eggs, milk and wool. Babies grow up!

Fish

Rivers, lakes and the sea each hide their own catch. Click the moment "!" appears.

Mine & explore

Dig deeper floors as your skill rises, or brave the huge Wilds and its outposts.

Craft

Kitchen, smithy, mill and more: turn raw goods into tools, food and gear.

Earn

Deliver Task Board orders for gold and tokens, trade at the Market Hall.
